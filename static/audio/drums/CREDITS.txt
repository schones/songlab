Each file is one hit from one microphone of the kit (16-bit mono, 44.1 kHz):

  kick.flac   Samples/kick_mic/ks_vl6_rr1.wav    kick, velocity layer 6 of 6 (the hardest),   0.40 s
                                                 kick mic
  snare.flac  Samples/snare_mic/sc_vl5_rr1.wav   snare centre hit, layer 5 of 6, snare mic    0.26 s
  hat.flac    Samples/oh_mic/hc_vl3_rr1.wav      closed hi-hat, layer 3 of 4, overhead mic    0.20 s
  stick.flac  Samples/oh_mic/ss_vl3_rr1.wav      snare sidestick, layer 3 of 4, overhead mic  0.15 s
              (the count-in's stick click)

Processing, the same for all four: start 1 ms before the onset (the first
sample over 5 % of the peak) with a 0.5 ms fade-in; cut to the length above;
raised-cosine fade-out over the last 50 / 40 / 40 / 30 ms; normalised to a
peak of -1 dBFS; encoded losslessly as FLAC. No EQ, compression or resampling.
The relative levels are set in drums.js (DRUM_SPECS), not in the files.
