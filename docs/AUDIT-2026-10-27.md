- `review_page.py` is hard-wired to I Got Rhythm (key :38, meter :126, title :254/:273, `--audiveris` required).

- Schema needs an explicit reduction rule for song-sheet piano parts (RH doubles the voice with chords under it): melody = voice staff; comp = RH below melody + LH chords; bassLine = LH bass. `voicingSource` field planned but not in schema.md.

IGR scan is a 1975 reprint with editorial chords — contradicts SOURCES.md:13 "no later reprints"; record the caveat in `attribution`. What Is This Thing Called Love has no scan.

**Proposed order:** finish I Got Rhythm → Embraceable You (clean source, ukulele chords as a free cross-check) → But Not for Me (same publisher/format) → Body and Soul (best for voicings, hardest). Fallbacks: Honeysuckle Rose, Sweet Georgia Brown.
